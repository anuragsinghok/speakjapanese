Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

JLPT Spin (jlptspin.com) is a free Japanese speaking-practice website run by Anurag Singh. This page explains what data the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: there are no accounts, we only store your email if you give it to us, and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you sign up for the free JLPT study schedule. It is used to send you occasional study tips and updates about JLPT Spin.
  • Anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics: pages viewed, approximate country/city, device and browser type, how you arrived (for example from Instagram), and actions on the site such as spins, timer use and shares. This helps us understand what learners find useful. IP addresses are anonymised.
  • Settings saved on your own device (browser local storage): your chosen JLPT level, timer length, and whether you have already signed up. This never leaves your device.

We do not collect your name, password, payment details, voice or video. Audio playback uses your device's own text-to-speech; nothing you say is recorded.

2. Services we use

  • MailerLite — stores the email list and sends emails. MailerLite privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics — usage statistics, using cookies. How Google uses data. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting) and Cloudflare (domain/DNS) — these may keep standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers.

3. Unsubscribing and deleting your data

Every email has an unsubscribe link — one click and you're off the list. To have your email deleted completely, send a DM on Instagram to @anurag.pov and it will be removed within 30 days.

4. Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works fully without them.

5. Children

The site is suitable for all ages, but the email list is intended for people aged 13 and over.

6. Not affiliated

JLPT Spin is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT), the Japan Foundation, or JEES.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions? DM @anurag.pov on Instagram.
