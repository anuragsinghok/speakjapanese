JLPT Spin

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

JLPT Spin (jlptspin.com) is a free Japanese speaking practice site that I, Anurag Singh, run as a personal project. This page explains what data the site collects and what you can do about it. In short: there are no accounts, I only have your email if you give it to me, and I will never sell it.

1. What gets collected

  • Your email address, only if you sign up for the free study schedule. I use it to send you study tips and updates about JLPT Spin once in a while.
  • Anonymous usage stats from Google Analytics. This includes which pages are viewed, your rough location (country or city), your device and browser, how you found the site (for example Instagram), and things like spins, timer use and shares. It helps me see what's actually useful. IP addresses are anonymised.
  • Settings saved in your browser, like your JLPT level, timer length and whether you already signed up. These stay on your device.

The site does not collect your name, password, payment details, voice or video. The audio uses your phone or computer's built-in voice and nothing you say is recorded.

2. Services the site uses

  • MailerLite stores the email list and sends emails. MailerLite privacy policy
  • Google Analytics counts visits and uses cookies. How Google uses this data. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and Cloudflare handles the domain. Both may keep normal server logs.
  • Google Fonts is used to load the fonts.

3. Unsubscribing or deleting your data

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. If you want your email deleted completely, DM me on Instagram at @anurag.pov and I'll remove it within 30 days.

4. Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart. You can block or delete them in your browser settings and the site will still work fine.

5. Age

Anyone can use the site, but the email list is meant for people aged 13 and over.

6. Not the official JLPT

JLPT Spin is a personal project. It isn't connected to or approved by the official Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT), the Japan Foundation or JEES.

7. Changes and contact

If I change this policy I'll update the date at the top. For any questions, DM me on Instagram at @anurag.pov.
